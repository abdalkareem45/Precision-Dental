🎯 Why CAL is the Golden Diagnostic Standard Over PD

1. Probing Depth is an Unstable Measurement: PD measures from the Free Gingival Margin (FGM) to the pocket base. The gingival margin moves with edema, hyperplasia (drugs like Amlodipine), or surgical shrinkage. A 5mm PD can be a false pocket with 0mm bone loss!
2. The CEJ is an Anatomical Constant: The Cementoenamel Junction (CEJ) never shifts. Measuring attachment loss from the CEJ directly records the irreversible loss of supporting alveolar bone and Sharpey's periodontal ligament fibers.
3. The Deceptive Recession Trap: With 4mm of recession and a 2mm pocket, PD is normal (2mm), but true attachment loss is severe (6mm CAL). Only CAL reveals the true destruction!
